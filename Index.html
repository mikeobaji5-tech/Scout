Sender rotation Recommended for large lists

When sending to large lists (5,000+), sending too many from one address risks it being flagged. Set a limit and the tool will pause automatically and remind you to switch to your next sender account in Thunderbird before continuing.

e.g. 100 means pause every 100 sent.
Update your sender name on the Compose step. You'll be reminded to switch accounts in Thunderbird at each pause.
